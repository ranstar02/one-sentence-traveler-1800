One sentence explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — using only words a person from 1800 would already know, and one single picture they could hold in their head.

> Think of a road joining every house on earth, down which a letter flies faster than any horse and an answer comes straight back.

The sentence is 128 characters: one road, one letter, one answer — the whole internet as a traveler from 1800 could picture it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the sentence (one line, one sentence, at most 200 characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
